We use DSS, Siril or AutoStakkert for stacking our frames. We see many options for stacking and just go for the recommended settings. Just to understand how they would affect our final image, here's a small exercise. There are 5 light frames, try out different stacking methods, change iterations etc. 
For your reference, the methods are average, median, kappa-sigma clipping, and median-kappa-sigma-clipping.

Try to use numpy for stacking, but if it gets too complex, go for other libraries.

Just as a fun exercise, you can try to detect stars and get their m_inst so that the codes for the previous questions are tested for the generalisation.

For the methods, refer to [DeepSkyStacker](http://deepskystacker.free.fr/english/index.html).
